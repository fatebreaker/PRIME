A: Antibodies against postsynaptic nicotinic cholinergic ion channels, B: Autoantibodies against the presynaptic voltage-gated calcium channels, C: Autoimmune demyelination of peripheral nerves, D: Blockade of presynaptic acetylcholine release at the neuromuscular junction, E: Lower motor neuron destruction in the anterior horn

### Response:
Make a list of key neurological symptoms and their progression. Identify potential causes of neuromuscular weakness, focusing on conditions that cause flaccid paralysis. Evaluate the patient's autonomic symptoms, such as dry mouth and constipation, to refine the differential diagnosis.
